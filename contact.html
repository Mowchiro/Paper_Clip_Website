How do I place an order?

Simply click on a product and enter the amount of items you want to buy before proceeding to add it to your card then checkout.

Can I modify or cancel my order after placing it?

Yes, it will be listed on the pending order under your shopping cart and can be modified and cancelled unless its been shipped.

Will I receive an order confirmation?

After checking out a product, the customer will receive a notification and it will be listed on the transaction history.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept e-wallet payments, and cash on delivery.

Do you offer cash on delivery?

Yes, PaperClip offers cash on delivery.

Can I place bulk or corporate orders?

This depends on the stock the shop owner lists on their product listing. If it isn't available, pre-ordering items can be done as well.

Do you offer discounts or promo codes?

PaperClip offers discounts and promo codes monthly that can be claimed.

Where do you ship?

We only currently deliver products in the Philippines.

How long does delivery take?

Around 3-7 business days after processing checkout.

How much does shipping cost?

Shipping fee varies by location and will be shown along the product at checkout. Do you offer free shipping? - Yes, we offer free shipping codes from claimable promo codes.

How can I track my order?

Theres a tracking button for your orders that can be found on your shopping cart or here in the help center.

What happens if I miss a delivery?

If your miss a delivery, the courier will attempt to redeliver it or contact you through your listed phone number.

Can I change my shipping address after placing an order?

ou can only change your shipping address before the order is shipped.

Why is my order delayed?

Delays may occur due to weather, holidays, or courier issues.
