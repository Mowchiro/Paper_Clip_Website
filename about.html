ABOUT US


"The secret of getting ahead is getting started." — Mark Twain

A static HTML website revolving around the world of an online store that sells stationery items. This is a project for our Introduction to Web Programming where we were mostly taught HTML and CSS in creating a website. In Paper Clip, you will find your usual E-commerce pages such as the home page that greets you with quotes or pictures to entice the consumer into consuming the products we want to sell. You can also find the Log In, or Sign up options for new users, this in theory is where the system would store the user's transactions, orders, favorites, added to cart products, etc. Third is the most important part of the page, the shopping section. The shop section includes various advertisements from different brands and maybe some ongoing sales that the user might be interested in, it's also where they can find the products listed. Theres also a contact page for user feedback, contact, and customer services. Lastly, theres an admin for store owners and such where they can track the list of orders, profits, etc. That's all for my website, it's completely static and serves no functions as of the moment since we have yet to learn how to add the backend to our websites. :D

A well-crafted shop of trinkets and stationary for those who write & dream of a better world.
